# 👩🏻‍⚕️ Medical Cost Personal Dashboard

## 📚 About Data
This project analyzes the Medical Cost Personal Dataset to explore how demographic and health-related factors are associated with medical insurance charges.

## 💡 Highlights
- Overall Charges: Average charges are $13,270.42, with a median of $9,382.03, suggesting a right-skewed distribution.
- Smoking Status: Smokers have approximately 3.8 times higher average charges than non-smokers.
- Age: Average insurance charges generally increase with age.
- BMI: Individuals in the obese BMI category have the highest average charges.
- Sex and Region: Males have slightly higher average charges, while the Southeast has the highest regional average.
- Combined Factors: Smokers have higher average charges across all age groups and regions analyzed.

## ✏️ Data Wrangling
Conducted simple data wrangling and data cleaning:
1. Missing Values: Checked for missing values. No missing values were found.
2. Duplicate Rows: Identified and removed 1 duplicate row.
3. Data Types: Checked the data types of numerical columns (age, children, and charges
4. Categorical Values: Verified consistency in sex, smoker, and region.
5. Numerical Ranges: Checked the ranges of numerical variables for implausible values.
